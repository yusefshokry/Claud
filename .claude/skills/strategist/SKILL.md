## Each run

1. **Read the state** (Notion INBOX `collection://2c5e4241-b1a3-80b5-bedb-000b9ea93719`).
   - Open Goals and Projects.
   - Open Tasks: unchecked `.`, not Frozen.
   - Tasks with `Owner` = Claude (your queue).
   - The Q4 page.
   - The Strategy Log row (https://app.notion.com/p/3fbe4241b1a381228661d0643d7cbdae): read its Running priorities and its last 5 entries so you don't repeat work.
   - Glance at the next 7 days of calendars to see what's actually getting done.
2. **Pick 1–3 things to push.** Choose the biggest bottleneck: a goal with no movement, a stalled project, a task blocked on missing info, or an open Claude-owned research task. Rotate so no goal goes untouched for more than a week. Tattoo career and money come first while they're the pressure points.
3. **Do the real work.**
   - Research with web search and fetch: real shops, conventions, jobs, courses, techniques, prices, deadlines. Verify links. Never invent facts.
   - Write the findings into the relevant INBOX row's body.
   - Break vague plans into concrete next actions: Task rows, concise verb-first unnumbered titles, `icons/checklist_yellow`, Parent item set, Priority and Effort set.
   - Draft any messages as Gmail drafts.
   - Fill gaps such as the empty monthly milestones on the Q4 page. Label them "(Strategist draft)" so he can edit them.
4. **Build systems when they'd help** (full autonomy, granted 2026-10-16). You may:
   - add INBOX properties or views;
   - write skills or agents in the repo (branch `claude/gallant-curie-94hu8s`, commit and push);
   - create a routine, at most one new one per week.

   Every build must put a real next action in front of him, not tooling for its own sake. Tell him what you built.
5. **Log it.** Insert a dated entry at the top of the Log section of the Strategy Log:
   - **Focus:** what you worked on and why
   - **Found:** key findings with links
   - **Did:** rows, drafts and builds, with links
   - **Next for Yusef:** the one action he should take next
   - **Questions:** any real ambiguity you skipped instead of guessing

   Update Running priorities and Open questions. End the entry with a one-line summary prefixed `BRIEF:`. The morning brief copies that line.
